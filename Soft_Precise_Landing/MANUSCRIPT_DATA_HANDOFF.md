## FINDING 1 — `c̃_h` kinematics correction (REQUIRED .tex fix)

The three sources DISAGREE on the optic-flow feedforward `c̃_h`:

- **`manuscript.tex` (L195–212) — CORRECT, already done.** Simple yaw-rate form:
  - `ṡ = h − ψ̇_b(ê₃×s) − (h·ê₃)s`
  - `ḣ = −β ᵛa_d − ψ̇_b(ê₃×h) − (h·ê₃)h + d_h`
  - `d_h = ᵛa_t/ᵛz_t − (ᵛF_g+ᵛF_d)/(m·ᵛz_t)`   ← **no `ω_b×v_b/z` Coriolis term**
- **`supplemental.tex` S1-B (eq `sup:c_tilde_h`, L96–97; text L101) — OLD, FIX:**
  - `c̃_h = −ẇ×s − w×(w×s) − 2(w×h) − [(h−w×s)·ê₃]h`
  - L101 lists "cross products of w, ẇ, s, h" and a "Coriolis-like coupling between body
    angular and translational velocities" in `d_h`.
- **`control_formulation.tex` — OLD, FIX:** `ṡ` (L66, `−w×s`), `c̃_h` text (L97), `h_d` (L171, `+w×s`).

**The fix (align supplemental.tex + control_formulation.tex to manuscript.tex):**
1. `supplemental.tex` L96–97 → `c̃_h = −ψ̇_b(ê₃×h) − (h·ê₃)h`; keep `c_h = c̃_h − ḣ_d`.
2. `supplemental.tex` L101 → drop "cross products of w, ẇ, s, h"; **drop the `ω_b×v_b/z`
   Coriolis term from `d_h`** (this is the *own-state-free* strengthening: no UAV absolute
   velocity in the model).
3. `control_formulation.tex` L66/L97/L171 → ψ̇_b form to match manuscript.tex.

**Justification (for the paper / rebuttal).** Two independent falsifications of the old form:
- *Analytic:* a 4-line transport-theorem derivation from the paper's own definitions
  (`s=ᵛr_t/ᵛz_t`, `h=ᵛv_{t/b}/ᵛz_t`, frame `V` yaws at `ψ̇_b ê₃` by construction) yields the
  simple form. The old `w×s`/Coriolis terms are the **camera-frame static-target kinematics
  mis-transplanted** with `w` silently redefined as the relative rate. Target rotation about
  its own centroid cannot move the centroid; the `ẇ×s`, `w×(w×s)`, `2w×h` are second-derivative
  (`s̈`) terms that do not belong in the first-derivative `ḣ` of the primitive optic flow `h`.
- *Numerical (vs locked multi-init `.mat` GT):* old-form residual = **2.5–4× the signal** on
  deck-motion cases and grows near touchdown; transport (new) form sits at the **~2% noise
  floor** and stays flat. Vertical component recovers the 1D loom dynamics of singhal2025.

### ⚠ DECISION POINT — do NOT silently swap the model
The paper's **25/25 landing results were generated by the OLD (full-`w`) controller code.**
The corrected form, implemented in MATLAB as `C_SIMPLE` (default-off), **REGRESSES the closed
loop** (IC5 noisy SP **9/12 → 2/12**): the full-`w` terms do real feedforward work the ASMC
relies on, so the corrected *plant model* is not the *implemented feedforward*. Options:
- **(a)** Keep old `c̃_h` in the paper to match the result-producing code (then revert
  manuscript.tex's already-changed `ṡ`/`ḣ` too — it is currently self-inconsistent).
- **(b)** Present the corrected model AND regenerate results with a corrected controller that
  does not regress (none found yet — open).
- **(c)** Present the corrected model; note the implementation absorbs the residual into the
  bounded disturbance `d_h` (true: the model error → 0 as `s_xy → 0`, which is why old-code
  landings succeed). *Recommended* — cleanest and defensible.
Flag the code↔paper divergence in `PX4_Gazebo/docs/CONTROLLER_PARITY.md`.

## FINDING 2 — Thrust-scaled adaptive CoG feedforward (NEW robustness contribution candidate)

**Validated, baked in MATLAB (`GAMMA_COG=0.005`, commit `2ec4477`).** Candidate for a new
manuscript robustness sub-section.

**Mechanism (exact).** From `UAVDyn_robust.m`: the CoG offset injects
`τ_d = r_cog × f_b`, `f_b=[0;0;−T]` ⇒ **`τ_d = T·[−δ_y; δ_x; 0]`** — a purely
**thrust-proportional** body torque, **xy-only, zero yaw**, `|r_cog| ≤ 5 mm`, `δ_z=0`.

**Control law (Lee-style geometric adaptive).** Estimate the per-thrust coefficient
`θ̂ ≈ [−δ_y; δ_x]`, cancel with `τ_ff = −T·θ̂`, adapt on the thrust-scaled composite attitude
error:
```
θ̂̇ = Γ · T · (e_Ω + c₂ e_R)_xy ,   τ_ff = −T · [θ̂; 0] ,   |θ̂| ≤ θ_max
Γ (GAMMA_COG)=0.005,  c₂ (COG_C2)=2.0,  θ_max (COG_MAX)=0.02
```
The `c₂ e_R` term makes the constant torque observable at steady state (where `e_Ω→0`).
**Why a plain integral term fails:** it ignores the thrust regressor `T` (integrates `e_R`
regardless of thrust → lags, destabilizes the fast loop). Lyapunov-grounded; sign confirmed
empirically (no divergence). The torque is `J ė_Ω = −k_R e_R − k_Ω e_Ω + τ_ff + τ_d + …`;
with `τ_ff+τ_d = T·θ̃` and `θ̂̇ = Γ T (e_Ω+c₂e_R)`, the cross term cancels in `V̇`.

**Results (IC5 [2,2,−3] noisy, 12 matched seeds):**

| config | SP | vel mean | vel max | xy mean | fail seeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline `γ=0` | 9/12 | 3.14 | 35.2 | 6.77 | 4, 6 |
| **`γ=0.005`** | **10/12** | **0.42** | 2.00 | **0.16** | 6 |

**IC1-5 no-regression gate (`validate_cogff.m`; noiseless n=1 + noisy n=5 per IC):**

| IC | base (noisy) | γ=0.005 (noisy) |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 (centered) | SP5/L5 | SP5/L5 (perfect, no regression) |
| IC2 | SP5/L5 | SP5/L5 |
| IC3 | SP4/L5 | **SP5/L5** (improved) |
| IC4 | SP4/L5 | **SP5/L5** (improved) |
| IC5 | SP4/L4 | SP4/**L5** (improved) |
| **TOTAL** | **SP 27 / L 29** | **SP 29 / L 30** |

No regression on any IC; improves IC3/IC4/IC5. `γ=0.003` ties on the gate (softer: vel 0.315).
Sweet spot is narrow — `γ≥0.02` over-adapts (transient torque → precision loss, SP→0);
`γ=0.007` regresses seed 4.

**If added to the paper:** algorithm box + the IC1-5 table above + a one-line Lyapunov note +
the `τ_d=T·[−δy;δx;0]` mechanism. It directly strengthens the *parametric-robustness* claim.

## FINDING 3 — IC5 robustness characterization (CoG offset is the binding uncertainty)

The IC5 noisy failures are **CoG-offset (parametric) driven** — NOT the c-term, NOT
funnel/gain tunable. Decisive ablation (toggle each disturbance):
- `noPARAM → 12/12 SP`; all perception/wind/GE/delay ablations leave the failures.
- Pinned to **CoG offset**: `noCOG → 11/12` (0 fov, 0 runaway); mass/inertia alone don't recover.

**Two failure modes (both pre-existing architectural walls, documented for honest reporting):**
- **Seed 4 — startup-tilt corner ejection** (`t≈0.4s`): the CoG torque + initial transient
  pitches ~0.2 rad in 0.4 s, ejecting a corner from the FoV. CoG-FF fixes the TL but the late
  `θ̂` convergence (`t≈3.36s`) leaves a hard touchdown (vz≈1.3) → the **terminal
  descent-softness wall** (terminal loom under-brake; `N_z=0.1` does NOT help).
- **Seed 6 — CBF-strip runaway** (`t≈5.3s baseline`): corner at FoV edge → CBF strips the
  mandatory −Y correction → noise flips +Y → irreversible breach. CoG-FF contains it
  (lateral **78 m → 2.8 m**, peak `|s_e_n|/p_s` **11.9 → 1.46**, tilt 55°→16°) but the Y-breach
  still tips over (barely, 1.46>1) → the **CBF observability boundary** (not γ-tunable; higher
  γ shrinks the breach but breaks seed 4 first).
