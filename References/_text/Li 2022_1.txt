ABSTRACT   
The quadrotor UAV is an aircraft with excellent performance such as vertical take-off and landing, hovering in the air, 
low-speed flight, etc. It is flexible and easy to operate. Therefore, the application of quadrotor UAVs in the military and 
civilian fields such as aerial photography, express transportation, plant protection, pipeline inspection, etc. has shown 
unique advantages. The application scenarios are being rapidly developed and have broad prospects. The control system 
of the quadrotor UAV has the characteristics of multi-variable, high coupling, nonlinear, and underactuated, which 
brings challenges to the design of the controller. For this reason, this paper established the dynamics model of the "X" 
quadrotor UAV, designed an adaptive controller based on backstepping, and completed simulation experiments. The 
simulation results show that in the presence of disturbance, the designed adaptive controller still has good control 
accuracy, response speed and robustness, which proves the feasibility of adaptive control in the field of quadrotor UAV. 
Keywords: quadrotor, flight control, backstepping, adaptive control 
1. INTRODUCTION  
The quadrotor UAV is a six-degree-of-freedom aircraft with excellent performance such as vertical take-off and landing, 
hovering in the air, and low-speed flight. It has simple structure, small size, light weight, flexible maneuverability, good 
concealment, and simple operation features [1]. At the same time, the control of quadrotor UAV has the characteristics of 
multi-variable, high coupling, nonlinear and underactuated. Therefore, the design of the control system of the UAV is 
very challenging [1]. In the past two decades, researchers have noticed these characteristics of quadrotor UAV and 
designed various controllers using many different control techniques. It was finally found that the traditional linear 
control method is difficult to maintain effective control of the UAV under extreme conditions such as large attitude angle, 
sudden load, and large disturbance [2]. However, some nonlinear control techniques are not suitable for UAVs due to the 
huge amount of computation. 
With the rapid development of microelectronic technology and electromechanical technology, the computing power has 
been revolutionized, which makes the nonlinear controller have the possibility of practical application in the field of fast 
response control. In the field of UAV flight control, modern control technologies such as backstepping control [2],[3],[6], 
sliding mode control [9], adaptive control [4],[5], fuzzy control [7], and artificial intelligence control [8],[11] are also 
developing rapidly. When the quadrotor UAV is in an environment with external disturbance, and this disturbance 
changes with time or cannot be accurately measured, the constant gain controller will not be able to compensate for this 
disturbance, and the control effect is unsatisfactory. At this time, the adaptive control technology can be used to solve the 
disturbance problem and achieve a good control effect [1]. Due to some special reasons, some parameters of the 
quadrotor UAV cannot be accurately measured or change slowly, such as mass, moment of inertia, length of the arm, etc. 
At this time, adaptive control can deal with this dilemma [1]. 
In this paper, a dynamic model is established for the "X" quadrotor UAV, and the model is appropriately simplified. Then, 
an adaptive controller is designed by using the backstepping method, and a simulation experiment is carried out. 
Through the analysis of the simulation results, the correctness of the established dynamic model and the effectiveness of 
the designed controller are proved. 
2. DYNAMIC MODELING AND SIMPLIFICATION OF QUADROTOR UAV 
The control system of the quadrotor UAV is a typical multivariable, highly coupled, nonlinear system. And because the 
system has six degrees of freedom of motion, but only four control inputs, so it is a typical underactuated system. 
Because of the high complexity of the system, it is particularly important to establish an accurate model and design a

5. CONCLUSION 
In this paper, the dynamic model of the quadrotor UAV is established, the adaptive controller based on the backstepping 
method is designed, and the corresponding simulation experiments are completed. The experimental results shows that 
the backstepping controller has the advantages of high control accuracy, fast response speed, and small overshoot, but 
there are problems that the disturbance error does not converge and the robustness is poor. Compared with the 
backstepping controller, the designed adaptive controller not only has the same advantages, but also the disturbance error 
converges rapidly, greatly improves the robustness. These illustrate the feasibility and advantages of adaptive control in 
the field of quadrotor UAVs. Due to the limitation of time conditions, this paper does not verify the designed adaptive 
controller through flight experiments, and subsequent research can complete the flight experiment and further optimize 
the controller based on the experimental results.
