ABSTRACT 
This paper takes the autonomous landing of four-rotor UAV as the specific research object. Aiming at the problem of 
identifying the feature points of landing signs and improving the accuracy of autonomous landing, an improved multi-
objective soft subspace clustering algorithm is proposed. The anti-redundant mutation operator and forward comparison 
operation are designed, it is proposed to improve the population diversity and convergence speed of the algorithm. The 
iNSGA-II is used as the base algorithm, and the repair operator and local search operator are designed to reflect the 
clustering characteristics. An autonomous landing control model based on visual recognition is established. A large number 
of position feature points are clustered and analyzed to decouple the highly nonlinear mapping relationship in the feature 
space, and then the redundant information between the dimensions is stripped to achieve the maximum stability of the 
sampling points, the importance sequence of all features and the accurate classification. 
Keywords: quadrotor UAV, autonomous landing, improved multi-objective soft subspace clustering algorithm, anti-
redundancy mutation operator 
 
1. INTRODUCTION 
With the advancement of vision technology, quadrotor drones have been rapidly developed and widely used. Due to its 
small size, low cost, and strong mobility, UAVs are favored by military, civilian, and other research fields [1]. The core 
problem of autonomous landing of quadrotor drones is how to realize target position recognition and position calculation, 
and then control the flying attitude of drones in real time to meet the requirements of autonomous and precise landing. 
Traditional UAV autonomous landing navigation methods mainly include: Global Positioning System (Global Positioning 
System, GPS) navigation landing [2,3] Inertial Navigation System (Inertial Navigation System, INS) landing and INS/GPS 
integrated navigation and other methods [4 -6] Visual navigation landing methods include pure visual navigation and landing 
systems and vision-based multi-sensor fusion navigation and landing systems [7-9]. By simulating the autonomous landing 
process of the UAV, setting the coordinate system of the UAV landing, and cooperating with the algorithm, the selection 
of the landing target is realized, and the entire landing process is completed. It is highly operable and worthy of promotion. 
However, in the case of long distance, dim light or small pitch angle, the landing target recognition is not very successful 
[10]. Therefore, it is necessary to study a clustering algorithm with limited recognition range and accurate target position 
recognition. 
2. SOFT SUBSPACE CLUSTERING ALGORITHM 
Subspace clustering algorithm is a spatial clustering method based on spectral clustering [11,12]. Clustering is to divide 
similar sample features into different subsets by static classification method, so that the member objects in the same subset 
have similar attributes (the samples in the class are closely distributed), and the distance between different subsets is farther. 
Through the feature selection technique, it is represented by its feature information mapping in multiple low-dimensional 
subspaces, that is, the weight representation method, and the subspace under the weight representation is the soft subspace. 
The soft subspace clustering algorithm can not only deal with the dimensionality reduction problem of high-dimensional

The soft subspace clustering algorithm has been widely used in the field of artificial intelligence [13]. Image processing in 
machine vision is an important research field of artificial intelligence [14-17], and it is also one of the development priorities

cases, pilots are still used for remote control landing, but when the environment does not allow or multiple drones need to 
be controlled, drone autonomous landing technology is particularly important. The promotion of drone autonomous 
landing technology can save manpower It can also solve the landing under special circumstances. Therefore, the study of 
UAV autonomous landing technology is an indispensable link in the UAV operation system. 
3. IMPROVED SOFT SUBSPACE CLUSTERING ALGORITHM 
The vision module in the machine vision control system includes industrial computer, industrial camera, line laser, optical 
filter and vision structure shell, and the improved multi-objective soft subspace clustering is applied on this module. First, 
according to the four-rotor UAV's five flight attitudes: translational motion, pitching motion (circling Y), rolling motion 
(circling X), yaw motion (circling Z), and vertical motion, Taking the distance from the target and the measurement of the 
offset angle as the optimization objects, the objective function of the closeness of the sample distribution within the class 
is established, a hybrid measurement algorithm is proposed, and an update strategy in the iterative clustering process is 
deduced; Secondly, using the theory of complex systems, the optimization function based on the Euclidean distance metric 
criterion is mapped to the Gaussian kernel space, and the second objective function is established; Then use iNSGA-II as 
the base algorithm, design the repair operator and the local search operator to reflect the clustering characteristics, and use 
the standard data on the network to verify the feasibility of the improved multi-objective soft subspace clustering; Finally, 
based on the machine vision and autonomous landing control model, using the distribution information of the images 
sampled by the airborne camera on each feature, the experiments verify that the algorithm has the performance of 
dimensionality reduction, redundant data removal, and fast convergence speed.

3.2 Forward Comparison Operation 
When sorting in non-dominated sorting individuals, the design of the forward comparison operation is to avoid redundant 
non-dominated frontiers, so that it has an acceleration effect on all test problems and greatly improves the running speed 
of multi-objective optimization. The forward comparison operation is to compare the dominance relationship between the 
currently visited structure set individual and the nearest non-dominated individual. In the frontier stratification, if the 
cumulative number of samples of non-dominated individuals exceeds the population size, stop the non-dominated sorting, 
otherwise, continue to execute.

4．APPLICATION of AUTONOMOUS LANDING of QUADROTOR UAV 
In this paper, the industrial computer is used as the carrier of the control module. Its function is to provide the basis for the 
huge data processing and information interaction of the vision module and the control module, and realize the processing 
of the control program. During the landing process, the landing target data is obtained through the vision module, and then 
sent to the control module, while the pose detection sensor is built into the quadrotor UAV, which can detect the angle 
information of the UAV's flight attitude in real time, that is to say, the Motor speed v and UAV flight speed V are used as 
response values, and motor speed v, flight height h and quadrotor UAV flight angle are used as input variables to establish 
a mathematical model of autonomous landing control parameters. 
5. CONCLUSIONS 
In this paper, the control model of the control unit, vision unit and peripheral sensing and driving unit is established, In the 
drive module, in order to realize the precise measurement and attitude control of various flight attitudes of the quadrotor 
UAV, Introduce the cosine angle measure into the objective function of the closeness of the sample distribution within the 
class, Combining Min's distance to build a mathematical model is a new perspective for UAV landing target image 
recognition. According to the new objective function, the update strategy in the iterative process is deduced, which can 
better realize the purpose of dimensionality reduction, de-redundancy and de-noising for the landing position image 
processing.
