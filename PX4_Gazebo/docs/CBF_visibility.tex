\noindent\emph{Scope.} Visibility is an \emph{attitude} constraint --- the body tilt moves the field of
view --- so we guarantee it with a small QP (a clamp) on the desired tilt, on the \emph{real} camera
plane. The coupling between tilt and feature is the \textbf{rotational part of the IBVS interaction
matrix}, $\Lw$, evaluated at the measured image point: it is exact, \emph{depth-independent}, and needs
no de-rotation / virtual frame. The barrier uses image features only --- scale- and depth-free (no
$\beta$, no optic flow, no disturbance term). Notation follows the manuscript.

\section{Barrier on the real camera plane}
Per image axis $k$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:barrier}
\boxed{\,h_k=\varphi_{\max,k}-|\,^\Cframe\hat r_k|-\delta_k\,}.
\end{equation}
$h_k\ge0\iff$ the marker (outermost feature point) is on the sensor. $\,^\Cframe\rhat$ is the
\emph{measured} camera image point --- nothing is de-rotated.

\section{Tilt$\to$feature coupling: the rotational interaction matrix}
A camera rotation moves a feature point by the rotational part of the IBVS interaction matrix, a standard
and \textbf{depth-independent} object evaluated at the measured point $\,^\Cframe\rhat=(x,y)$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Lw}
\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}=\Lw(\,^\Cframe\rhat)\,\bm\omega_{\rm rp}+\bm d,\qquad
\Lw=\begin{bmatrix} xy & -(1+x^2)\\ 1+y^2 & -xy\end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}
where $\bm\omega_{\rm rp}$ is the body roll/pitch rate (the control) and $\bm d$ is the exogenous
(translation / target) drift. $\Lw$ contains \textbf{no depth} --- the tilt coupling needs no $\beta$ and
no virtual frame. The drift is measured directly by stripping the rotational term from the observed
feature velocity, $\bm d=\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega_{\rm rp}^{\rm curr}$, so it carries
only the genuine target motion, not our own tilting.

For a finite tilt change the exact map is the rotation homography of the measured point; the
first-order form $\,^\Cframe\rhat(\bm\theta)\approx\,^\Cframe\rhat+\Lw\,(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})$ is
the local linearization and replaces the earlier (incorrect) $\,^\Cframe\rhat=\,^\Vframe\rhat+\tan\theta$.

\section{The visibility filter: a QP over the body tilt}
With $m_k=\varphi_{\max,k}-\delta_k-\tau\dot\delta_k$ the predicted margin,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qp}
\boxed{\;\bm\theta^\star=\arg\min_{\bm\theta}\ \|\bm\theta-\bm\theta_d\|^2
\ \ \text{s.t.}\ \ \big|\,^\Cframe\hat r_k+[\Lw(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})]_k+\tau d_k\big|\le m_k,\ \ |\bm\theta|\le\theta_{\rm cap}\;}
\end{equation}
A small 2-variable QP (the two tilt axes), with $\le2$ binding rows plus the cap --- closed-form. $\Lw$ is
near-diagonal for a centred marker (the $xy$ off-terms vanish), so it is nearly a per-axis clamp; the
off-diagonal carries the genuine roll$\leftrightarrow$pitch cross-coupling near the FoV edge.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Directional} --- only the outward binding row is active; inward (recovery) and tangential
tilts are free. No strangling.
\item \textbf{Input-aware} --- the $\theta_{\rm cap}$ box never demands a tilt the inner loop cannot hold.
\item \textbf{Forward-invariant} --- the look-ahead uses the predicted margin in $\tau$\,s: the target
drift $\bm d$ (left) and the marker-fill rate $\dot\delta$ (right) shrink it, so the box tightens early
and forces the inward tilt ($\dot h\ge-\alpha(h)$). $\tau=0$ gives the static clamp.
\end{itemize}

\section{Two-phase $\delta$ (central-marker convention)}
The designated \emph{central} marker is the target. \textbf{Phase 1 --- while it decodes, $\delta_k=0$}:
the barrier is centroid-only, keeping the target centre in frame while deliberately letting the central
marker grow and overflow as the UAV closes on it (its visibility \emph{is} the guarantee; using its large
extent would fire the barrier early). \textbf{Phase 2 --- on its overflow} (decode-fail, hysteresis-gated
against flicker $\Rightarrow$ touchdown range), $\delta$ \textbf{ramps in} over a few frames from the
visible markers' $\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}_k$ and tracks them thereafter. The ramp (not a step) keeps $h$ from
dropping discontinuously at engagement, but clamp the ramp gain so $\delta$ never \emph{lags} the measured
$\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}$ --- a ramp slower than the fill under-protects. Within Phase 2 the visible markers
themselves leave (a mild inner sawtooth); $\dot\delta$ is reset across those switches.

\section{Feasibility floor and hand-off}
The QP \eqref{eq:qp} is \textbf{infeasible} iff the predicted margin is gone ($m_k<0$ --- the marker fills
the frame within $\tau$) or even the full $\Lw\theta_{\rm cap}$ tilt cannot bring $\,^\Cframe\rhat$ inside.
Then visibility is physically impossible $\Rightarrow$ raise the perception-floor flag and hand off to the
texture-free ring flow.

\section{Assumptions (and the practical challenge each carries)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$\Lw$'s sign / axis convention is calibrated.} Its \emph{form} is the standard interaction
matrix, but the body-rate$\to$image-axis sign (roll$\to$which axis, which direction) depends on the camera
mount. \emph{Must-do:} command a small tilt, check $\Delta\,^\Cframe\rhat$ matches $\Lw$ in sign, before
trusting the barrier.
\item \textbf{The filtered tilt is realized} (SO(3) loop tracks $\bm\theta^\star$). \emph{Challenge:}
attitude bandwidth $\Rightarrow$ lag; $\theta_{\rm cap}$ bounds magnitude, not delay.
\item \textbf{The drift $\bm d$ is the translation part} ($\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega^{\rm
curr}$) and $\dot\delta$ the loom. \emph{Challenge:} finite differences $\Rightarrow$ filter + binding-axis
hysteresis; the disturbance enters as \emph{measured} motion --- no separate $\bm d_h$ term.
\item \textbf{First-order $\Lw$ over the cycle.} Exact is the rotation homography of $\,^\Cframe\rhat$; the
$\Lw(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})$ linearization is accurate over one control step. At $\theta_{\rm
cap}=60^\circ$ the integral (homography) is the safe choice for the boundary $\bm\theta$.
\item \textbf{$\theta_{\rm cap}$ is the true deliverable tilt} (thrust unsaturated).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scale-freeness and depth-freeness}
The barrier \eqref{eq:barrier}, the coupling \eqref{eq:Lw}, and the box \eqref{eq:qp} contain only the
measured image point $\,^\Cframe\rhat$, $\delta$, $\bm\varphi_{\max}=\mathcal R/(2f)$, the body rate, and
the tilt. \textbf{$\Lw$ is depth-independent by construction} (no $\mathcal V z_\mathrm{t}$, no $\beta$),
so the whole formulation is manifestly scale- and depth-free. The only metric quantity upstream is the
geometric tracker's $R_d$ (a controller output).

\section{Implementation (per control cycle)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(once)} Calibrate $\Lw$'s sign / axis convention (Assumption 1).
\item Measure $\,^\Cframe\rhat$ (board centroid, homography). Two-phase $\delta$: $0$ while the central
marker decodes; on overflow (hysteresis) ramp in the visible markers' $\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}$.
\item Evaluate $\Lw(\,^\Cframe\rhat)$; form the drift $\bm d=\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega^{\rm
curr}$ and $\dot\delta$ (filtered).
\item Predicted margin $m=\varphi_{\max}-\delta-\tau\dot\delta$; build the QP \eqref{eq:qp}.
\item $\bm\theta_d$ from $R_d$ (equivalently the desired lateral accel via $\tan\theta_d={}^\mathcal I a_{d,xy}/a_z$);
solve $\to\bm\theta^\star$ (a 2-var clamp).
\item If infeasible, raise the perception-floor flag and switch the flow source to the ring estimator;
else pass $\bm\theta^\star$ (equivalently the clamped $a_{xy}$) to the SO(3) inner loop.
\end{enumerate}
