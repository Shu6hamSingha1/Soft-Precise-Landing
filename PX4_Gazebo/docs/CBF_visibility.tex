\noindent\emph{Scope.} Visibility is an \emph{attitude} constraint --- the body tilt moves the field of
view --- so we guarantee it with a small QP (a clamp) on the desired tilt, on the \emph{real} camera
plane. The coupling between tilt and feature is the \textbf{rotational part of the IBVS interaction
matrix}, $\Lw$, evaluated at the measured image point: it is exact, \emph{depth-independent}, and needs
no de-rotation / virtual frame. The barrier uses image features only --- scale- and depth-free (no
$\beta$, no optic flow, no disturbance term). Notation follows the manuscript.

\section{Barrier on the real camera plane}
Per image axis $k$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:barrier}
\boxed{\,h_k=\varphi_{\max,k}-|\,^\Cframe\hat r_k|-\delta_k\,}.
\end{equation}
$h_k\ge0\iff$ the marker (outermost feature point) is on the sensor. $\,^\Cframe\rhat$ is the
\emph{measured} camera image point --- nothing is de-rotated.

\section{Tilt$\to$feature coupling: the rotational interaction matrix}
A camera rotation moves a feature point by the rotational part of the IBVS interaction matrix, a standard
and \textbf{depth-independent} object evaluated at the measured point $\,^\Cframe\rhat=(x,y)$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Lw}
\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}=\Lw(\,^\Cframe\rhat)\,\bm\omega_{\rm rp}+\bm d,\qquad
\Lw=\begin{bmatrix} xy & -(1+x^2)\\ 1+y^2 & -xy\end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}
where $\bm\omega_{\rm rp}=[\omega_\phi,\omega_\theta]$ is the body roll/pitch \emph{rate} and $\bm d$ is the
exogenous (translation / target) drift. $\Lw$ contains \textbf{no depth} --- the tilt coupling needs no
$\beta$ and no virtual frame. The drift is measured directly by stripping the rotational term from the
observed feature velocity, $\bm d=\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega_{\rm rp}^{\rm curr}$ (using
the true gyro rate $\bm\omega^{\rm curr}$), so it carries only the genuine target motion, not our own
tilting.

\paragraph{Lean vector vs.\ rotation axis.}
The QP variable $\bm\theta$ is the \emph{lean-direction} vector --- the horizontal direction of the
desired thrust/acceleration, $\bm\theta_d=R_z(-\psi)\,{}^\mathcal I\!a_{xy}/a_z$ --- not the body
angular-rate vector that drives \eqref{eq:Lw}. A lean toward $+x$ is produced by a rotation about the
$+y$ axis, so the two differ by a fixed $90^\circ$ rotation,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:M}
\bm\omega \;=\; M\,\bm\theta,\qquad M=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The feature therefore moves with the \emph{effective} coupling $\widetilde\Lw\equiv\Lw M$ (validated
against an independent pinhole$+$attitude camera model: $<8\%$ over the near-hover envelope). For a
finite tilt change the exact map is the rotation homography of the measured point; the first-order form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lin}
\,^\Cframe\rhat(\bm\theta)\approx\,^\Cframe\rhat+\widetilde\Lw\,(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})
=\,^\Cframe\rhat+\Lw\,M\,(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})
\end{equation}
is the local linearization, accurate over one control step. (The drift term keeps $\Lw\bm\omega^{\rm
curr}$ unchanged --- $\bm\omega^{\rm curr}$ is a measured \emph{rate}, already in the rotation-axis frame,
so no $M$ is applied there.)

\section{The visibility filter: a QP over the body tilt}
With $m_k$ the predicted margin (see Sect.~\ref{sec:twophase}),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qp}
\boxed{\;\bm\theta^\star=\arg\min_{\bm\theta}\ \|\bm\theta-\bm\theta_d\|^2
\ \ \text{s.t.}\ \ \big|\,^\Cframe\hat r_k+[\widetilde\Lw(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})]_k+\tau d_k\big|\le m_k\;},
\qquad \widetilde\Lw=\Lw M
\end{equation}
A small 2-variable QP (the two tilt axes), with $\le2$ binding rows, solved by a few projection
iterations onto the rows of $\widetilde\Lw$. $\widetilde\Lw=\Lw M$ is near-diagonal for a centred marker
(at $\,^\Cframe\rhat=0$, $\Lw=\left[\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right]=M^{-1}$, so
$\widetilde\Lw=I$ --- a clean per-axis clamp, closed-form there); the off-diagonal carries the genuine
roll$\leftrightarrow$pitch cross-coupling near the FoV edge, which the iterations resolve.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Directional} --- only the outward binding row is active; inward (recovery) and tangential
tilts are free. No strangling.
\item \textbf{Input-aware} --- after the box projection converges, apply the radial saturation
$\bm\theta^\star\leftarrow\bm\theta^\star\cdot\min\!\big(1,\,\theta_{\rm cap}/\|\bm\theta^\star\|\big)$
so the commanded tilt is always within what the SO(3) inner loop can hold. Applying the cap
\emph{inside} the projection loop would interfere with the box constraint's convergence; the
post-solution saturation preserves the deliverability bound without creating infeasibility.
\item \textbf{Forward-invariant} --- the look-ahead shifts the anchor by $\tau\bm d$, so the QP accounts
for centroid drift over the next $\tau$\,s in both phases.
In \textbf{Phase~2}, the predicted margin is additionally shrunk by $\tau\dot\delta_{\rm eff}$, forcing
the inward tilt before the fill rate consumes the remaining headroom ($\dot h\ge-\alpha(h)$).
In \textbf{Phase~1} ($m_k=\varphi_{\max}$), the $\tau\dot\delta$ term is excluded by design: the marker
is allowed to grow, so only the centroid-drift look-ahead $\tau\bm d$ is active.
$\tau=0$ gives the static clamp.
\end{itemize}

\section{Two-phase $\delta$ (central-marker convention)}\label{sec:twophase}
The designated \emph{central} marker is the target. The predicted margin $m_k$ is phase-dependent:

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Phase 1 --- central marker decodes.}
$\delta_k=0$, so $m_k=\varphi_{\max,k}$ (full half-FoV).
The barrier is centroid-only: the QP box spans the entire image plane, and only fires if the
\emph{centroid} drifts toward the edge. The central marker is deliberately allowed to grow and
overflow as the UAV closes in --- its visibility \emph{is} the guarantee; using its large half-extent
would fire the barrier early. $\dot\delta$ is tracked but excluded from $m_k$ in Phase~1.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Phase 2 --- central marker overflows} (decode-fail, hysteresis-gated against flicker
$\Rightarrow$ touchdown range). $\delta_{\rm eff}$ \textbf{ramps in} over a few frames from the
visible-\emph{set} half-extent ($\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}_k$ over ALL visible markers' corners, so
$|\,^\Cframe\rhat|+\delta_{\rm eff}$ is the true outermost feature --- not one marker):
\[
  m_k = \varphi_{\max,k} - \delta_{{\rm eff},k} - \tau\dot\delta_{{\rm eff},k}.
\]
The ramp (not a step) keeps $h$ from dropping discontinuously at engagement, but clamp the ramp
gain so $\delta_{\rm eff}$ never \emph{lags} the measured $\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}$ --- a ramp slower
than the fill under-protects. Within Phase~2 the visible markers themselves leave (a mild inner
sawtooth); $\dot\delta$ is reset across those switches.

\medskip
\noindent\emph{Implementation:} hysteresis threshold~$=3$ consecutive decode-fails before Phase~2
activates; ramp completes over~$5$ control cycles (${\approx}120$\,ms at 42\,Hz).
Both $\delta_{\rm eff}=\alpha\,\delta_{\rm ref}$ and $\dot\delta_{\rm eff}=\alpha\,\dot\delta_{\rm ref}$
are scaled by the same ramp factor~$\alpha\in[0,1]$, where $\delta_{\rm ref}$ and $\dot\delta_{\rm ref}$
are the half-extent and loom rate measured at the last successful decode.

\section{Feasibility floor and hand-off}
With $\theta_{\rm cap}$ applied outside the QP, the box constraint \eqref{eq:qp} is \textbf{infeasible} iff
$m_k<0$ for some~$k$ (the marker fills the frame within $\tau$). In Phase~1, $m_k=\varphi_{\max,k}>0$
always, so the QP is \textbf{always feasible} while the central marker decodes.

The perception-floor hand-off is therefore triggered by \emph{central-marker overflow} (Phase~2
activation, hysteresis-gated), not by QP infeasibility. On Phase~2 entry, or if $m_k<0$ under
Phase~2 margins, raise the perception-floor flag and hand off to the texture-free ring flow.

\section{Assumptions (and the practical challenge each carries)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The coupling $\widetilde\Lw=\Lw M$ and its sign convention are calibrated.} The QP variable
is the lean vector $\bm\theta$, but $\Lw$ couples the rotation-axis rate; the fixed $90^\circ$ map
$\bm\omega=M\bm\theta$ \eqref{eq:M} bridges them, so the QP uses $\widetilde\Lw=\Lw M$. Any residual
mount-dependent sign of $\Lw$ is a one-time check: command a small tilt and confirm
$\Delta\,^\Cframe\rhat$ matches $\widetilde\Lw$ in sign before trusting the barrier.
\item \textbf{The filtered tilt is realized} (SO(3) loop tracks $\bm\theta^\star$). \emph{Challenge:}
attitude bandwidth $\Rightarrow$ lag; $\theta_{\rm cap}$ bounds magnitude, not delay.
\item \textbf{The drift $\bm d$ is the translation part} ($\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega^{\rm
curr}$) and $\dot\delta$ the loom. \emph{Challenge:} finite differences $\Rightarrow$ filter + binding-axis
hysteresis; the disturbance enters as \emph{measured} motion --- no separate $\bm d_h$ term. The homography
centroid (hence $\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}$) must stay \emph{smooth across marker-set changes} (the
homography re-fits on a different visible subset) or $\bm d$ spikes --- filter / hold a stable subset across a switch.
\item \textbf{First-order $\Lw$ over the cycle.} Exact is the rotation homography of $\,^\Cframe\rhat$; the
$\Lw(\bm\theta-\bm\theta_{\rm curr})$ linearization is accurate over one control step. At $\theta_{\rm
cap}=60^\circ$ the integral (homography) is the safe choice for the boundary $\bm\theta$.
\item \textbf{$\theta_{\rm cap}$ is the true deliverable tilt} (thrust unsaturated).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scale-freeness and depth-freeness}
The barrier \eqref{eq:barrier}, the coupling \eqref{eq:Lw}, and the box \eqref{eq:qp} contain only the
measured image point $\,^\Cframe\rhat$, $\delta$, $\bm\varphi_{\max}=\mathcal R/(2f)$, the body rate, and
the tilt. \textbf{$\Lw$ is depth-independent by construction} (no $\mathcal V z_\mathrm{t}$, no $\beta$),
so the whole formulation is manifestly scale- and depth-free. The only metric quantity upstream is the
geometric tracker's $R_d$ (a controller output).

\section{Implementation (per control cycle)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{(once)} Calibrate $\Lw$'s sign / axis convention (Assumption 1).
\item Measure $\,^\Cframe\rhat$ (board centroid, homography). Advance the two-phase $\delta$ state:
if central marker decodes, reset Phase~2 counters and set $\delta_{\rm eff}=0$; otherwise increment
the decode-fail counter, and once the hysteresis threshold is reached, ramp $\delta_{\rm eff}$ toward
the visible markers' $\tfrac12\mathrm{ptp}$.
\item Evaluate $\Lw(\,^\Cframe\rhat)$; form the drift $\bm d=\,^\Cframe\dot{\rhat}_{\rm obs}-\Lw\bm\omega^{\rm
curr}$ and $\dot\delta_{\rm eff}$ (filtered; zero in Phase~1).
\item Two-phase predicted margin: Phase~1: $m_k=\varphi_{\max,k}$; Phase~2:
$m_k=\varphi_{\max,k}-\delta_{{\rm eff},k}-\tau\dot\delta_{{\rm eff},k}$.
If $m_k\le0$ for any $k$, raise the perception-floor flag and hand off to the ring estimator.
Otherwise build the FoV-constraint QP \eqref{eq:qp}.
\item $\bm\theta_d=R_z(-\psi)\,{}^\mathcal I\!a_{d,xy}/a_z$ (the lean vector from the desired lateral accel);
solve \eqref{eq:qp} with $\widetilde\Lw=\Lw M$ $\to\bm\theta^\star$ (pure FoV-box alternating projection,
always converges when $m_k>0$). Apply post-QP deliverability cap:
$\bm\theta^\star\leftarrow\bm\theta^\star\cdot\min\!\big(1,\theta_{\rm cap}/\|\bm\theta^\star\|\big)$.
\item \textbf{Apply $\bm\theta^\star$ directly as the desired attitude.} Build the desired body-$z$ axis
from the safe tilt, $\bm r_{d3}=\big[-R_z(\psi)\bm\theta^\star,\,1\big]/\big\|[\,\cdot\,,1]\big\|$, and
pass $R_d$ to the SO(3) inner loop, taking the thrust \emph{magnitude} from the descent loop. This avoids
re-encoding $\bm\theta^\star$ as $a_{xy}=a_z R_z(\psi)\bm\theta^\star$ and low-pass-filtering it: $a_z$
cancels in $-\,{}^\mathcal I\!a/\|{}^\mathcal I\!a\|$, so the direction is identical, but the accel-path
LPF (time constant $\tau_{ia}$) would smear the barrier's hard bound by several frames --- the
guarantee would then not land on the commanded attitude. The direct map makes the deliverable-tilt
guarantee exact.
\end{enumerate}
