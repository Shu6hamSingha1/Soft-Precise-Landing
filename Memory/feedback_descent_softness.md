**The hard-touchdown root (why landings were 3-4 m/s).** The loom reference `h_d_z = h_rd = -0.42` commands a descent velocity `v = |h_rd|*Z` — FAST at altitude (2.1 m/s at Z=5), slow at the deck. The drone builds up that velocity but **can't bleed it off in time**, because the only descent feedback (scale-free) is the loom `h_z = v/Z`, which is **poorly observable at altitude**: at Z=5 a 2.3 m/s descent gives `h_z≈0.13` (tiny) — the SMC is blind to the building velocity, so it's chronically under-braked. By the time the loom reveals it (`h_z`→-2 at Z≈1m, t≈1.75-1.9s) the drone is at 6+ m/s → late hard brake (`a_u_z`→-20) → hard impact. This is the `h_e_z` divergence at ~1.75s.

**`h_e_z` NEVER converges to zero — it's structural (1/Z observability), not a gain.** `h_e_z = h_z - h_d_z`; converging means `v = 0.42*Z` exactly. Instead: **early `h_e_z>0`** (under-descending — loom too small to see the velocity) → **late `h_e_z<0`** (overshoot). It crosses zero at ~1.85s but blows through. No operating point holds zero because the loom-only feedback can't regulate the velocity until it's too late. **Confirmed `E_z`/`Γ_z`/`XI2_z` (tracking gains) can't fix it** — they track a signal that's blind early.

## Softness levers (n=1 each unless noted; cut touchdown velocity 2.84 -> 1.06 m/s)
- **`KAPPA0_Z=1.0` (the bootstrap value — THE key) = z braking authority from t=0.** The Combo had reverted it to 0.25 (MATLAB), costing softness. =1.0 → vel 2.84→1.3-1.8. It also **prevents the `E_z=0.1` κ_z-ratchet** (κ_z starts adequate, doesn't hunt up to the cap).
- **`N_z=0.1` (from 0.05) = faster κ_z adaptation.** The descent is only ~2s, so κ_z is **adaptation-rate-limited** (never reaches its equilibrium `∝1/P`): with N_z=0.05 κ_z only climbed to 0.64, with 0.1 to 1.01 → more braking authority in time → vel 4.4→2.8. (My prediction "N is just a rate multiplier, can't matter" was WRONG — true only at equilibrium, false in a 2s descent.)
- **`E_z=0.1` (from 1.0) = crisp arrest BUT un-bounds κ_z ALONE** (E_z=0.1+κ0_z=0.25 → κ_z=2.98 near the 3.0 cap, violent). **Stable only PAIRED with κ0_z=1.0** (κ_z stays 1.06, vel 1.32). - **`P2INF_z=1.0` (from 1.5) → vel 1.06 (softest) + `h_e_z` term 0.82 (tightest).** Tighter z funnel floor raises the z barrier gain → tighter `h_e_z`. **It only became a lever once `XI2_z=0.6` was baked** (faster funnel contraction): `p_2_z(2s)=e^(-XI2_z*2)*(4-p₂∞_z)+p₂∞_z` → 1.90@floor=1.0 vs 2.25@floor=1.5.
