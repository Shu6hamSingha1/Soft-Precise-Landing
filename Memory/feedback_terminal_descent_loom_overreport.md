**What blocks the precise touchdown** (traced on a clean-baseline `aruco_board` landing 2026-06-11, with `IMG_RECORD` video + saved data; final xy 1.86m, min xy 0.0 @ alt 1.5m): a **close-range optic-flow OVER-REPORT below ~0.5m**, on all three signals at once. NOT a control-tuning problem.

## The descent control is CORRECT down to ~1m
The z-SMC drives the loom `h_z` → `h_rd=−0.42`, which gives a proportional descent (v = h_rd·Z ∝ Z). It tracks **perfectly**: at alt 0.99m the measured `h_z=−0.41` ≈ ref −0.42, implied rate −0.41 = ideal −0.414. So the descent law + loom perception are sound in the well-conditioned regime.

## Below ~0.5m everything over-reports → the terminal failure
1. **LOOM over-report → BALLOON (the binding one).** The corner loom `h_z` goes noisy + spikes (std 1.45, a +5.06 outlier at n_corn=4) and **over-reports ~4×**: at alt 0.21m it read −1.5 to −2.15 (implying v≈0.35 m/s) while the **actual descent (GT alt gradient) was only ~0.08–0.1 m/s** (near-ideal). The z-SMC, seeing "descending 4× too fast", drove `a_u_z`→−9 (hard upward brake) and the drone **ballooned UP 0.21→0.36m** instead of settling. So the drone literally STOPS descending and climbs — on a phantom loom.
2. **LATERAL over-report → over-tilt/drift.** 0.56m off at 0.2m → 1/Z-amplified `s_e_n`≈1.1, `h_xy`≈2.3, `h_d_xy`→4.5 → `a_u_xy` spikes to 42 → hard tilt → feeds the balloon + slides the drone 0.56→1.0m.
3. **DECODE-fail → marker LOST.** The balloon+drift pushes the board to the FoV edge → `n_corn` 32→0 → open-loop → off-center touchdown (1.86m, alt 0.09m).

**Root = the same close-range corner-perception breakdown, now confirmed on the VERTICAL axis (the loom).** Above ~0.5m all signals track; below, the corner optic flow over-reports on loom + lateral and the decode dies. It is NOT control lag, NOT a too-fast descent (actual ~0.1 m/s = ideal), NOT gain tuning.

## The RING divergence is the cleaner terminal loom
Within-Img_Data (same clock) comparison, terminal alt<0.5m: corner loom std **1.45** + a +5.06 spike + dies (n_corn→0); **ring divergence std 0.70, no spikes, n_ring 127–151 (4–6× the corner's 24–38), and still 19–21 stations at touchdown when n_corn=0.** The ring SURVIVES the decode-loss and stays sane.

## Per-axis is the key — opposite lesson from the lateral flow-switch
The **loom (divergence) is observable from a PLANAR target**, so the ring's radial-expansion estimate is a VALID vertical signal — unlike the **lateral**, where the ring has ~0 information ([[feedback_lateral_kappa_runaway]]). So:
- **Vertical/loom (z):** lean on the **ring** at the terminal — it's robust + survives. This is what would stop the balloon.
- **Lateral (xy):** keep the **corner** (ring can't see it); still 1/Z-limited.

This is the *opposite* of the failed `FLOW_NCORN_SWITCH` lateral experiment (which failed precisely because the ring has no lateral). For the **vertical loom the ring IS the right answer.**

## ⚠️ THRESHOLD LOWERED 12 → 4 (2026-06-13, GT-loom recheck — `RING_LOOM_NCORN=12` was CAUSING the h_z under-report)
`RING_LOOM_NCORN=12` had been baked as the code default (a "single-run, no-balloon" decision). A GT-loom recheck (via `tools/gt_optical_flow.py`, IC2 reps) found it was the **cause** of the measured-`h_z`-vs-GT divergence at ALTITUDE, not a fix:
- **`n_corn` collapses to ~4 by 4 m altitude** (ArUco decode-fail starts high), so the `n_corn ≤ 12` gate fired the ring-handoff across the WHOLE descent — not just the terminal it was designed for.
- **The ring loom reads ~0 at altitude** (the divergence is genuinely small there + flow-precision floor), WORSE than the corner loom (which tracked GT: corner −0.6…−2.2 vs GT −0.8…−2.8). The handoff threw away the better corner loom for the ~0 ring → the fused collapsed to ~−0.15.
- **Isolation n=1 (=12 vs =0 vs =4), `|fused|/|GTloom|` over alt 1–4 m:** `=12` **6%** (0.15/2.44, the divergence) · `=0` 65% (1.09/1.67) · **`=4` 87%** (0.60/0.69, best). The fused now follows the corner at altitude (n_corn 5/17 > 4) and only hands to the ring at the TRUE terminal (n_corn ≤ 4).
- **Decision (user): default lowered 12 → 4** in `src/img_data.py`. Caveats: the very terminal (<1 m, n_corn=0) still reads ~0 (no corners AND ring~0 in that rep — but no over-report balloon either, nothing to over-report); and the test rep still fly-away'd laterally (xy 10.25 m) = the lateral wall, which the vertical ring-loom can't touch (n=1 outcome is noise; the loom-tracking *measurement* is the trustworthy part). **The ring's genuine benefit (no terminal 4× over-report balloon) is real but only <~0.5 m — thresh=12 mis-applied it from 4 m.** LESSON: validate any flow-source switch against GT *by altitude* — a terminal fix gated on `n_corn` fires early because corners decode-fail at altitude.

## ⚠️ THE WRONG-SIGN FREEZE + FIX (2026-06-13, commit 4ac539f — EKF loom froze on a stale value)
Digging into why `h_e_z(measured)` had the WRONG SIGN at the terminal (GT plummeting −2.5 m/s, measured loom read **+0.6** → z-SMC saw `h_e_z>0` and commanded MORE descent into the deck). It was NOT a perception miscompute — the loom **stopped being computed and FROZE on a stale value**:
- The fused EKF loom only updates if `corner_ok` OR `ring_ok` fires; else it's a random-walk predictor (F=I) that **HOLDS the last value**. At the terminal: `corner_ok=False` (n_corn→0 decode-fail) AND `ring_ok=False` (the ring 6-DOF lstsq `V_v_ring` is **zeroed when ill-conditioned** under the lateral fly-away, line 668-670 → `_ring_ok` line 1020 requires `any(_vvr!=0)`). Neither updates → **freeze** at a +0.6 picked up from a GENUINE brief balloon (GT confirmed +0.3) right as the marker died.
- **Architectural gap:** the robust radial divergence `pure_div` (the "safety net" the whole `RING_LOOM_NCORN` design is premised on) was computed + logged but **NEVER fed to the fusion EKF** — the EKF consumes the lstsq `V_v_ring`, which is exactly what gets zeroed.
**FIX (commit 4ac539f, all env-tunable, on by default):** (1) `RING_LOOM_PUREDIV=1` — feed `pure_div` as a scalar ego-loom measurement when the lstsq is rejected; (2) `FLOW_HTVZ_PRIOR=1` — **anchor `h_tv[2]→0`** (R=`FLOW_R_HTVZ`=0.3): a ground target (stationary OR moving rover) doesn't change its own altitude, so the LOOM component of target velocity is ~0, while `h_tv[0:2]` (horizontal, the moving-rover signal) stays FREE. WITHOUT this prior, the ego-loom `h_tr[2]+h_tv[2]` is under-determined when corners die and the EKF parks the descent in `h_tv[2]` (h_tr=−1.06/h_tv=+1.06, sum=0) → **re-freezes h_tr** (the loom the controller reads); (3) anti-freeze decay (`FLOW_LOOM_STALE_MAX`=6, `FLOW_LOOM_DECAY`=0.85) — backstop for total signal blackout. **Verified n=1 IC2 (HtvzPrior):** longest frozen-h_tr run **45→3 frames**, `h_tv_z` **+1.06→~0**, terminal `h_tr_z` **93% correct-sign** (was wrong-sign). Numerically confirmed for BOTH stationary (frozen split −1.06→−0.08) and moving-rover (ego −2.0 → h_tr −2.02, h_tv_z→0.02). **RESIDUAL (honest):** when ALL loom sources die (corners gone + `pure_div`≈0 from radial-cancellation under fly-away), the loom correctly reads **~0 ("unobservable")** instead of freezing — the z-SMC then under-brakes, but that's the fundamental observability limit, curable only by keeping corners decoded (the lateral/perception wall), NOT the EKF. GT-verified throughout via `tools/gt_optical_flow.py` ([[reference_gt_optical_flow]]). n=5 IC1-5 gate pending.

**Why:** the terminal balloon is the binding "no precise touchdown" mechanism; it's perception (close-range loom over-report), not control.

The terminal descent fix = the **z-axis ring-weighted loom** (`RING_LOOM_NCORN`, default **4** since 2026-06-13 — was 12, which under-reported the loom 16× at altitude; see threshold-lowered section above).
