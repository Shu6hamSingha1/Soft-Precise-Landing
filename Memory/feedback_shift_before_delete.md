Don't delete or condense manuscript content in a single pass. First move sections/tables/figures to the supplemental intact. Only after the move is complete, optimize by removing duplicacies between main and supplement.

**Why:** User explicitly corrected the plan that proposed simultaneous deletion and moving. They want to preserve all content and review what's redundant afterward.

**How to apply:** Any manuscript compaction task should be two phases: (1) relocate to supplement with cross-references, (2) user-approved deduplication pass.
